# Sources and attribution

1. U.S. Government Publishing Office / Library of Congress, GovInfo BILLSTATUS XML for Congresses 113-118. Official bill identifiers, sponsors, cosponsor dates/withdrawals, actions, committees, related bills, text-version references, and CBO links. https://www.govinfo.gov/bulkdata/BILLSTATUS ; documentation https://github.com/usgpo/bill-status . Bulk inputs are archived separately with hashes, because official packages can later be corrected.
2. Clerk of the U.S. House of Representatives, individual official roll-call XML. Vote questions, results, member Bioguide identifiers, party labels, and positions. Each row preserves its exact source URL. https://xml.house.gov/ . Suspension passage requires two thirds of votes cast; see House Rule XV, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/HMAN-117/html/HMAN-117-pg701.htm . Legislative purpose, not Yea alone, determines interpretation.
3. U.S. Senate, official roll-call vote XML. Vote questions, results, member LIS identifiers, majority-requirement fields, party labels, and positions. https://www.senate.gov/votes/ ; procedural glossary https://www.senate.gov/about/research-tools/glossary.htm . Cloture is a procedure limiting consideration, not final passage.
4. @unitedstates/congress-legislators. Bioguide/LIS/ICPSR crosswalks, service terms, dated party affiliations where supplied, and committee assignments from historical Git snapshots. https://github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators . The current/historical roster files are pinned to the full commit recorded in `sources/github_current_commit.json`; committee observations have individually pinned commits. Upstream license is CC0-1.0, copied at `sources/roster/LICENSE`. Committee snapshots are observations rather than appointment-effective dates.
5. Lewis, Jeffrey B., Keith Poole, Howard Rosenthal, Adam Boche, Aaron Rudkin, and Luke Sonnet (2026). Voteview: Congressional Roll-Call Votes Database. https://voteview.com/ . Downloaded House and Senate member files for the 117th and 118th Congresses. Nokken-Poole estimates treat each Congress separately: https://voteview.com/articles/data_help_members . API/data-use guidance recommends direct data downloads: https://voteview.com/quota . Scores are model estimates, not direct measures of all political beliefs. Data-license status is recorded separately; do not assume the roster project's CC0 covers these files.
6. GovInfo BILLS XML, 1,383 unique text-version packages for 390 bill IDs with linked roll calls. https://www.govinfo.gov/help/bills . Each extracted version records the exact URL and source hash. Legislative-body extraction excludes metadata and form material; lowercasing and word/number tokenization remove punctuation, then unique five-word sequences are compared exactly.
7. CBO estimate metadata linked by official bill-status records. The actual estimate pages are referenced at cbo.gov but full estimate text and numerical scoring were not extracted here. Original bill-status source and CBO link are both preserved.
8. Four additional official documentary sources are listed in `data/DocumentaryEvidence118.csv`: a member's office statement, a House Democratic Caucus transcript, an Armed Services committees' negotiated-text statement, and an SSA legislative bulletin. These sources corroborate specific case facts and are attributed in the dossiers. Their presence does not independently validate every political explanation in them.

`data/SourceManifest.csv` identifies exact files, URLs, retrieval times, and hashes. Blank or missing data are not filled with zero. Full source archives are a separate optional download to keep the analytical data accessible.
